PUBLIC BETA • PRIVACY & DATA

Your creations stay yours.

Important: This beta does not provide accounts or cloud backups. Your work saves in your browser on your device. Clearing site data, switching devices or using a private-browsing session can erase it. Export backups often.

What the beta saves

Fursona Forge saves characters, uploaded portraits (resized for storage), worlds, lore, maps, commission-brief drafts, profile preferences and feature wishlists in browser localStorage. It does not intentionally send these creations to a Fursona Forge server. Your public-beta guest workspace is accessible only in the browser profile where you created it.

Backups and images

You can export your workspace as a JSON file from Profile & Backup, and import it on another device. Keep your backups somewhere safe; they may contain private writing and character art. Image uploads are resized, so also keep the full-resolution original art.

Network requests

This static beta loads website files from its web host and may request font files from Google Fonts. The hosting provider and external font provider may receive normal technical information such as IP address or browser details under their own practices. The beta does not include built-in analytics, ad tracking, account services, payment processing, or server-side messaging.

Artist previews and commissions

Artist profiles shown in the commission area are fictional examples. Writing a commission brief does not contact an artist or order artwork. No payment tools are built into this website.

Community and safety

Public galleries, accounts and direct messaging are not live. When these features are built, they will require separate privacy choices, moderation and reporting systems. Don't share passwords, personal contact details, addresses or private documents in a creation or commission brief you intend to share elsewhere. Only upload images you created or have permission to use.

Deleting your work

You can delete individual creations in the app. You can also clear this site's browser storage through your browser settings, which permanently deletes local content unless you have exported a backup. This beta does not have a cloud account to delete.
